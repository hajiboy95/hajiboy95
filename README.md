### 🌳 KiwiCanopy — the studio

Small tools that each take one tedious job off your plate. Easy the minute you
open them, powerful once you're ready — no tutorials required to get started.

- **[KiwiCV](https://kiwicv.kiwicanopy.com)** — a modular, ATS-optimized CV
  builder. Even sleek two-column layouts stay 100% machine-readable, so
  applicants focus on their story instead of fighting with margins.
- **[KiwiDesk](https://kiwidesk.kiwicanopy.com)** — a tiling window manager for
  macOS that arranges your windows for you. Mouse or keyboard, your call —
  quiet in the background and out of your way. 🥝

### 🤝 Built to be contributed to

I write the tools I open-source to be legible to humans **and** AI alike — clear
structure, honest docs, small readable pieces. The idea: whether you're a
seasoned dev or a first-timer with a good AI model at your side, you can land a
real PR. Easy things stay easy to help with; the depth is there when you want to
go further. Same philosophy as the apps — approachable by default, powerful on
demand.
